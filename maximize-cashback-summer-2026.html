Maximize Cashback This Summer 2026: Stack Rakuten + Chase Freedom for Double Rewards on Every Purchase

June 9, 2026 · 6 min read · Updated weekly

Most people leave hundreds of dollars on the table every year by using only one reward method at a time. The real trick? Stacking — layering cashback portals on top of rewards credit cards to earn double (or triple) on the same purchase. Here's exactly how to do it this summer.

Summer means more spending: vacations, back-to-school shopping, home improvement projects, and a surge in online orders. If you're not stacking cashback portals with a rewards card, you're essentially paying full price for things you could be getting a discount on — without any extra effort.

What Is Cashback Stacking and Why Does It Work?

Cashback stacking means using two or more rewards programs on the same transaction. It works because most cashback portals (like Rakuten) and credit card rewards programs operate independently — they track different parts of the same transaction. The portal earns its cashback based on your click-through from their site. Your credit card earns its rewards based on the payment. They don't conflict with each other.

The result: a single $100 purchase at a retailer like Macy's, Target, or Gap could earn you 3–5% from Rakuten's portal plus 1.5% from your Chase Freedom Unlimited card — a total of 4.5–6.5% back on a purchase you were already going to make.

💡 Quick Example: A $500 summer wardrobe purchase through Rakuten (4% cashback) paid with Chase Freedom Unlimited (1.5% back) = $27.50 back. Do that across a few summer purchases and you've earned a free tank of gas or a couple of nice dinners.

Step 1: Set Up Rakuten (Free, Takes 3 Minutes)

Rakuten is the largest cashback portal in the US, with partnerships at over 3,500 retailers including Amazon, Walmart, Target, Macy's, Nike, Sephora, and hundreds more. It's completely free to join and pays real cash (via PayPal or check) — not points with complicated redemption rules.

Once you sign up, install the free Rakuten browser extension. It automatically detects when you land on a partner retailer's site and activates your cashback with a single click — you never have to remember to visit Rakuten's site first. The extension also pops up with coupon codes automatically.

Current summer cashback rates at top retailers include 5–8% at many clothing and home goods stores, with elevated rates on select products during summer sales events. Rakuten also regularly offers double-cashback weekends on major retailers.

Step 2: Pair It With the Right Credit Card

Rakuten works best when paired with a flat-rate cashback card like the Chase Freedom Unlimited, which earns an unlimited 1.5% cash back on every purchase — no categories to track, no activation required. It has no annual fee, making it a set-and-forget baseline earner on every transaction.

Capital One Quicksilver is an excellent alternative to Chase Freedom Unlimited — also unlimited 1.5% flat cash back, no annual fee. Keep it as your Rakuten-paired card and you've got a clean, consistent 1.5% stacked on top of every portal payout.

Discover it® Cash Back is worth adding to your summer stacking arsenal for its 5% rotating quarterly categories. When the active category aligns with a Rakuten partner — like Amazon in Q4 or restaurants in Q3 — you can hit 6.5%+ combined. Discover also matches all cash back earned in your first year, effectively doubling your returns.

For grocery shopping specifically (including online grocery delivery from stores that don't run high Rakuten rates), the Amex Gold Card earns 4x Membership Rewards points at U.S. supermarkets — effectively 4% back when you redeem points for travel or cash. Pair that with any available grocery store cashback on Rakuten and you're maximizing every grocery dollar.

Step 3: Layer In Summer-Specific Bonus Offers

Summer is prime time for elevated cashback rates. Here's where to look:

Rakuten Summer Sales Events

Major retailers run summer clearance sales in June and July, and Rakuten typically bumps cashback rates during these windows — sometimes to 10–15% at select stores. Check the Rakuten homepage before any major purchase; rates change weekly.

Chase Freedom Unlimited Dining Bonus

The Chase Freedom Unlimited earns 3% back at restaurants — great for summer dining out. Stack that with OpenTable or similar dining rewards programs and you're earning on every meal.

Amex Offers

If you carry the Amex Gold, log in and check "Amex Offers" in your account — it regularly includes one-time statement credits at retailers (e.g., "Spend $50 at Gap, get $15 back"). These stack with Rakuten cashback for triple-dipping on a single purchase.

Best Retailers to Stack Right Now

Based on current Rakuten rates plus card bonuses, here are the highest-value stacking opportunities this summer:

Online clothing/apparel: Many major brands run 5–8% on Rakuten + 1.5% Chase = 6.5–9.5% total. Hotels: Book through Rakuten's travel portal for 4–7% + card points = significant savings on summer travel. Home improvement: Home Depot and Lowe's typically run 2–4% on Rakuten — useful if you're tackling summer projects. Amazon: Rakuten rates vary (typically 1–3%), but combining with Amazon's own rewards card can still net you 4–5% back.

How Much Can You Actually Save?

Real savings depend on your spending habits, but here's a realistic example for a typical American summer. Assume $2,000 in summer spending across clothing, home goods, dining, and a weekend trip. With Rakuten averaging 4% on applicable purchases and Chase Freedom Unlimited adding 1.5% on everything: that's roughly $110 in cashback on purchases you were already making. That's a free dinner for two, or a tank and a half of gas.

Power users who also use the Amex Gold for groceries, stack Amex Offers, and hit Rakuten double-cashback events regularly report saving $500–$800 per year — purely from stacking, with zero lifestyle changes.

🏁 Bottom Line

Cashback stacking is the simplest, highest-ROI money habit you can build. Sign up for Rakuten (free), install the browser extension, pair it with the Chase Freedom Unlimited for everyday purchases and the Amex Gold for groceries — and you'll be earning double rewards on virtually every dollar you spend online this summer.
