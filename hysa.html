What Is a High-Yield Savings Account?

A high-yield savings account (HYSA) works exactly like a regular savings account — your money is FDIC-insured and available whenever you need it. The difference is the interest rate. Online banks can offer higher rates because they don't have physical branches. There's no catch — your money is just as safe.

Smart Uses for a High-Yield Savings Account

Interest rates have been elevated through 2025–2026 compared to the near-zero years before. Economists expect rates to gradually ease — so now is a good time to lock in a strong HYSA. Best uses: Emergency fund (3–6 months of expenses), vacation or holiday savings, down payment savings, and any cash you'll need in the next 1–3 years.

What a High-Yield Savings Account Can't Do

A 5% savings account won't make you rich. It's not a replacement for investing. For long-term goals, you'll still want a 401(k), IRA, and index funds. HYSAs are for money you need to keep safe and accessible — not for your retirement nest egg.
